About CareFlow

A web-based demonstration of the Hospital Patient Management System abstract.

Project overview

CareFlow models how a hospital can organize patients waiting for consultation using two data structures: a normal FIFO queue and an emergency priority queue. It is inspired by the supplied abstract and intended for educational demonstration.

01

Queue (FIFO)

Normal patients are processed in the order they arrive.

02

Priority Queue

Emergency patients are ordered by assigned priority; level 1 is highest.

03

Patient records

Patient ID, name, age, medical problem, category, priority and status.

Important limitations

  • This is a front-end prototype, not a production hospital information system.
  • Data is stored locally in the current browser using localStorage.
  • No authentication, server, database, doctor management, billing, or appointment booking is implemented.
  • Do not enter real patient-identifiable or confidential health information.
  • Priority settings are illustrative and do not replace medical triage or clinical judgement.
